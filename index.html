The Tenant Opt-Out Process
For a tenant who already has a homeowner’s or renter’s policy that covers stored property.

They have 10 days from move-in. They upload their insurance declaration page at MyOwnPolicy.com, and it has to show all five of these:

  • An accepted insurance provider (major carriers only)
  • Their name exactly as it appears on the lease
  • A current expiration date — not expired, and not expiring within 30 days
  • Personal property coverage or an off-premises endorsement, clearly stated
  • The deductible amount, clearly shown

Anything missing and it won’t be approved. If nothing is approved within the 10 days, the tenant stays on the $2,000 Protego plan and the charge appears on their invoice. Once an opt-out is approved, the Protego charges come off their account.

Trouble with the upload? They can email the declaration page to support@myownpolicy.com — that team responds within one business day.
